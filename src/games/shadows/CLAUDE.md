# Schatten finden

Route `/schatten/`.

A picture looks for its shadow among 2–4 black ones (the same emoji with `filter: brightness(0)`); tap the right shadow or drag the picture onto it, or choose with arrow keys + Enter / 1–4. A wrong shadow shows its colours for a moment ("Das ist die Giraffe."). Pictures and texts in `content.ts`, by theme (Tiere, Fahrzeuge, Essen, Dinge). „Wer schaut da raus?“ (idea X-04, `PeekPlay.tsx`, a card of "Was gehört zum Tier?" that shares the stage, pictures and glyphs with the shadows): only a part of an animal peeks out of a bush (top, left or right) or the pond (`hideouts.tsx`), the child taps the whole animal among 2–4 below, and it comes out; tapping the bush rustles it and the animal makes its noise. The emoji is drawn on a canvas and cut to its outline (`glyph.ts`), so the part that shows is the same on every system.

## Languages

Both games speak the app language (German, English, Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, Arabic or Russian, `useAppLanguage()`): start screen and „Über das Spiel“ texts inline with `t({ de, en, zh, hi, es, ar, ru })`, game texts through `pick(SHADOW_TEXTS, language.id)` (`ShadowTexts` in `content.ts`), speech in `language.speechLang`. Each other language is a `content.<lang>.ts` (en, zh, hi, es, ar, ru), listed in `src/content/texts/texts-<lang>.ts` (which fills `SHADOW_TRANSLATIONS`): the pictures' names by id in the shape its grammar needs – English and Spanish `many` for plural words („scissors are“, „las tijeras buscan“), Spanish the article („del elefante“); Hindi the oblique form before की (कछुए की) and sentences without gendered verbs („… की परछाईं कहाँ है?“, „यह … है।“); Arabic `female` for the verb and pronoun (يبحث عن ظله / تبحث عن ظلها) and `the` where ال can't simply go in front; Russian the genitive for „Вот тень слона!“ and `many` („ножницы ищут“).
- Translations come only when the user asks. A new or changed German line comes German-only; add the other languages only on request. Gaps are marked in `src/content/ideas/languageStatus.ts`.
- When a German line changes its meaning, delete its translations in the same change (a stale translation is worse than German).
- A picture without its name in a language is said entirely in German (the language's texts function takes the whole German sentence, and `say` gives a sentence without the language's letters to the German voice, `voiceFor`). `shadows.test.ts` checks that no id is stale, that the languages translated so far name every picture, that Russian, Chinese, Hindi and Arabic have no Latin letters, and that the fallback works.
- Sentences must not use the past tense or depend on the picture's gender where the language can avoid it (no „нашёл/нашла“, no „его/её“; Hindi without रहा/रही); use the stored forms.
- Never show `item.name` directly; it is German. Use `texts.name(item)`.
