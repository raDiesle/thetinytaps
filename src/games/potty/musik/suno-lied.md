# Warte-Lied: what to type into Suno

The waiting song (`musik-toepfchen-warten-<lang>.mp3`) plays while the animal sits on the
potty and repeats until the tap that says „fertig“, so it has no beginning or end of its own.
It is sung by the one who sits („ich“, so the child can sing along, nothing that changes for boy or girl),
cheerful, with a part to shout back, and long (about three minutes), so it doesn't come round again too soon.
Every line is about going to the potty itself: sitting there without a nappy, being big now, Mama and Papa going to
the toilet too, waiting as long as it takes, and the cheering when it worked – never side stories (toes, sneezes,
animals) that have nothing to do with it (the user's choice). It never pushes: waiting is fine, „Pipi oder Kaka“
comes when it comes. The song never sings the thought bubble's stand-ins (water, rain, fountain, balloon,
ketchup, the hen, play dough) or „drücken“: those are the bubble's job. Create → Custom, Instrumental off.

Suno adds an intro and an ending anyway: when converting, cut the file from the first beat of
the first verse to the end of the last turnaround (on the measured beat grid, `bun run song`), so
the whole file loops without a gap.

## Deutsch (`de`, written 26 Oct 2026, rewritten 27 Oct 2026: all about going to the potty and waiting; made 27 Oct 2026 → `musik-toepfchen-warten-de.mp3`)

What the child sees while it plays: Tapsi on the potty, Mama and Papa clapping softly, a clock ticking on the wall.
„Kaka“ like everywhere else in the game.

```text
[Verse 1 – ukulele, woodblock ticking]
Ich sitz auf meinem Töpfchen,
die Windel ist schon weg!
Ich bin jetzt groß, ich kann das,
ich sitz am richt'gen Fleck.

[Chorus – glockenspiel joins]
Tick, tack, wir warten,
wir haben ganz viel Zeit.
Pipi oder Kaka –
bald ist es so weit!

[Verse 2 – ukulele, light shaker]
Die Mama geht aufs große Klo,
der Papa auch, na klar!
Und ich, ich hab mein Töpfchen –
das ist doch wunderbar!

[Chorus – glockenspiel]
Tick, tack, wir warten,
wir haben ganz viel Zeit.
Pipi oder Kaka –
bald ist es so weit!

[Verse 3 – playful, kazoo answers]
Mein Teddy trägt noch Windeln,
der ist ja noch so klein.

„Ich muss mal!“ – ganz allein.

[Chorus – glockenspiel]
Tick, tack, wir warten,
wir haben ganz viel Zeit.
Pipi oder Kaka –
bald ist es so weit!

[Bridge – call and response, the same voice shouts back, woodblock]
Wer braucht keine Windel? (Ich!)

Wer ist schon so groß? (Ich!)

[Verse 4 – softer, calm]
Manchmal geht es ruck-zuck,
und manchmal dauert's lang.
Ich bleib ganz ruhig sitzen,
die Uhr tickt ihren Gang.

[Chorus – glockenspiel]
Tick, tack, wir warten,
wir haben ganz viel Zeit.
Pipi oder Kaka –
bald ist es so weit!

[Verse 5 – bouncy again, kazoo]
Und wenn es dann geklappt hat,
dann klatschen alle mit,
dann abziehn, Hände waschen –
das ist mein Töpfchen-Hit!

[Chorus – glockenspiel, everything together]
Tick, tack, wir warten,

Pipi oder Kaka –

[Instrumental turnaround – 2 bars, woodblock tick-tock, leads back to Verse 1]
```

Style of Music:

```text
playful cheerful children's song, 100 BPM, F major, warm cheerful voice singing clearly, ukulele, glockenspiel, woodblock ticking like a clock, kazoo answers, light shaker, bouncy and silly but never hectic, simple repeating melody, same tempo all the way through, no intro, no fade out, no big ending, about 3:00
```

Exclude Styles:

```text
rap, heavy drums, electric guitar, distortion, choir, sad, fast, tempo changes, long intro, fade out, final chord
```

Title:

```text
Warten auf dem Töpfchen
```

## Русский (`ru`, written 27 Oct 2026)

The German song in Russian, verse for verse, same tempo, key and instruments, so the loop is measured the same way.
Sung by the one who sits, in the present tense and without anything that changes for boy or girl: no „я большой“, no
past tense of „я“ („расту“, „умею“, „сижу“ instead), the shout-back answers are just „Я!“. „Пи-пи“ and „ка-ка“ as
Russian parents say to toddlers. Not yet read by a native speaker.
