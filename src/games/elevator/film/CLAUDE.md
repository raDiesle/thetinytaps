# Films („🎬 Filme“, `film/`, G-14)

A story told like television, from place to place with a cut in between, instead of one world that scrolls along. Each scene (`Scene` in `types.ts`) is one place: its drawing, its `focus` (the box that must be seen, zoomed into the room above the strip; the drawing goes on round it, so other screen shapes see more, never an empty edge) and its `beats`, which happen one after the other – by themselves, or after a tap (`tap`, with the `ask` said while it waits). A tap anywhere sets off the waiting beat; where it should go is marked by the shared `TapHint` (a golden ring and a drawn hand pointing at it; the hand only before the first tap and again with the question after `NUDGE_MS` without a tap, and the longer the child waits the clearer the hint). A tap while a beat is still going on is kept when the scene has a tap beat to come: the next spot glows for a moment and the beat goes on by itself. Any key taps too. After its beats a scene may have a `choice`: two or three options, each a part of the picture (the cat in the tree, the burning house, the car in the ditch) or a round picture sign. The picture dims and the options come out of it one after the other as cards (`ChoiceCard`): each a frame of film with its holes, showing a close-up of that trouble (`Option.card`, the town drawing cut to it, `townPart`), flying out of its place in the picture with its sound (`Option.sounds`) while its line is said; once all are out the question is asked and the hint's drawn hand (`HintHand`) points at the first. The cards glow, side by side in the middle, clear of the ⏸ (`CARD.clear`); on a computer a small key cap shows 1–3. A tap on a card (or on the thing in the picture, or 1–3, as soon as its card is out) picks it: the others fall away, the picked card comes to the middle and grows, and the film goes on that way. Every option is right. A card is a still frame: nothing in its svg moves. Between scenes a dark iris closes and opens on the next place. Along the bottom a strip of small pictures shows the scenes of the film so far: past ones solid, the one playing framed in yellow, the next one dashed; at the end the voice says the closing sentence and a soft glockenspiel rings out (`playGlockenspielEnd`, G-31 – never a Tusch or cheer after a film, for every film) while everybody in the last picture who stands or cheers waves goodbye instead, one hand swaying slowly a few times and then resting (`FilmOver` from the engine turns their pose into `wave` in `Kid` and `Grownup`; who walks, eats or holds something goes on; nothing rises – no hearts or stars, the trophy is the end's celebration; `films.test.tsx` checks every way's last picture), the strip runs through once, then the shared `GameEnd` with the film's trophy. A ⏸ in the top left corner (a pale disc like the ✕ across, until the end buttons come) pauses the film: every wait of the script is held (`later` keeps what is left of it), every animation in the picture is paused (`getAnimations`, the CSS ones and `Go`'s), the voice is hushed and the background music stops; a veil with a big ▶ covers the picture, and a tap on it or any key goes on – neither counts as a tap of the film. A new wait in the engine goes through `later`, never a bare `setTimeout`, or the pause misses it.
- `types.ts` – the format: `Film` with `scenes(picks)`, which gives the scenes up to the next unanswered choice (or all to the end) for the picks so far; `Beat`, `Choice`, `Option`, `Spot`, `Box`, `around`.
- `FilmPlay.tsx` – the engine (iris, beats, taps, choices, hint, strip, party, end). `films.ts` – the films (`FILMS`), which `../cards.tsx` adds to `CARDS` after the trail levels and `../ElevatorGame.tsx` lists as modes of the kind „🎬 Filme“.
- `move.tsx` – `Go` moves a thing along a path when its path changes (the first path only places it), `route(step, …)` picks the path for a step; so a scene draws where everything is at each step and `Go` animates in between. A scene's `step` is 0 before its first beat and `j + 1` while beat `j` plays (and after it, until the next): so what moves in the first beat moves at step 1, never at step 0, whose path only places it (found building „From grain to bread“, whose scenes first moved one beat early or not at all).
- `people.tsx` – the grown-ups (the crew, a neighbour, a resident, a driver), drawn with the children's poses; a `Look`
  with `animal` is one of the app's mascots (the shared `MascotHead` instead of face and hair, paws instead of hands,
  `lashes` for a woman): the crew is a fox and a she-bear, the neighbour a vixen, the resident a bear, the postwoman of
  „Ein Brief für Oma“ a she-bear, the baker of „Beim Bäcker“ a bear (`wear={["baker"]}`: white jacket and baker's hat). The child and its own family (Mama or Papa, Oma) stay people until D-19 is decided –
  a human child with a fox mother doesn't fit; grown-ups in a role or next door may be foxes and bears; `places.tsx` – houses, trees, the road, the ditch; `truck.tsx` – the fire engine (ladder, blue light, wheels). The child is `Kid` from `../kid.tsx` with the `fire` outfit (helmet, jacket, boots).
- `krankenwagen.tsx` – „The ambulance“ and its `Ambulance` drawing (white box, red cross, blue lights, wheels); `Grownup`'s `wear` also takes `"rescue"` (orange jacket, silver stripes).
- `korn.tsx` – „From grain to bread“ with its `Tractor`, `SeedDrill`, `Trailer`, `Combine`, `Windmill` and the growing grain (`grainPaths`, paths built once at module level); `Grownup`'s `wear` also takes `"farmer"` (dungarees and a straw hat). It borrows from the other films what is the same place: `Bakery`, the baker and the oven and breakfast scenes from `baecker.tsx` (told again with its own English lines by `retold`), `RainCloud` from `garten.tsx`.
- `haus.tsx` – „We build a house“ with its `Digger`, `Mixer`, `TowerCrane`, `Van` and the growing `NewHouse` (hole, slab, rows of bricks, bare roof, tiles, door and windows); `Grownup`'s `wear` also takes `"builder"` (a yellow safety vest and a `HardHat`), and `Wheel` comes from `korn.tsx`.
- `muell.tsx` – „The rubbish truck“ with its `Bin` (lid, wheel, what lies in it by kind), the `Truck` (drawn facing left and turned round, so the arm's swing over the roof is one rotation round `PIVOT`; the bin rides in the arm while it is lifted) and the recycling yard's `Container`s.
- `film.css` – classes and keyframes `fm-` (`fm-br-` for „Ein Brief für Oma“, `fm-bk-` for „Beim Bäcker“, `fm-gt-` for „Unser Garten wächst“, `fm-kn-` for „From grain to bread“, `fm-hs-` for „We build a house“, `fm-mu-` for „The rubbish truck“); a duration that goes with the story is written `calc(… * var(--fm-pace))`.
- `films.test.tsx` checks every way through every film: it ends, a pick only adds scenes after those seen, no scene twice, two or three different options, every tap spot and option lies wholly in its scene's `focus`, every tap beat asks, every line is a German sentence, the pace is calm (at most three sounds in a beat, at least 0.9 s apart as played, a beat that speaks at least 2.7 s), and the last picture waves goodbye, never cheers.

A calm film: no siren, soft sounds.
- „Beim Bäcker“ (G-20, G-25, `baecker.tsx`, classes `fm-bk-`): in front of the bakery the child opens the door, the bell rings and the baker (a bear) asks it in; it picks what to bake by tapping it in the shop window (rolls, a pretzel, a cake) → in the bakehouse, with a baker's hat, it pours the flour into the bowl, steps up to the dough on the board and kneads it with both hands (`KneadArms`, the child's pose `knead`: three times it leans in, presses the dough flat and away and folds it back), then the dough, still small, rests and grows under a cloth, lifts the cloth, and the dough gets its shape → it opens the oven, the tray goes in, behind the door's glass the goods turn brown, the timer rings, it takes them out and warm steam rises → at home the table is laid, the goods go on the board and everybody eats. A calm film: soft sounds, the oven's warmth shown by its glow.
- „Unser Garten wächst“ (G-30, `garten.tsx`, classes `fm-gt-`): in the garden the child and Papa walk to the empty bed;
  on the table lie three seed packets and the child picks what shall grow (sunflower, carrots, strawberries) → at the
  bed it digs a hole with the spade, the seeds fall in from the packet, earth goes over them and the empty packet goes
  on a stick beside them, then it waters them (one tap each) → it fetches the rain cloud (rain on the bed), a sprout
  comes up, it pushes the cloud away and the sun shines, the plant grows from picture to picture → the end goes with the
  pick: the sunflower's bud opens and a bee visits it; or the child pulls out a carrot, holds it up and bites it while a
  bee flies to the tulips; or the strawberry flowers, a bee visits them, they turn into red berries, the child picks them
  into a bowl and eats one. A calm film: patience and growing, soft sounds (`playBuzz` and `playMunch` are recordings).

Rules for films (from building the first one; follow them in the next):
- **A film is calm, never a flood of stimuli** (user feedback, November 2026: a child of two is overwhelmed by sounds, voice and clicks close together). The engine does most of it, so keep it: `PACE` (`types.ts`) stretches every beat, sound cue, `Go` move, the ladder and the story's CSS (`--fm-pace`) – write times at a natural pace and never work round it with shorter ones. A line is never cut off by the next: the engine waits until the voice is done and then a quiet moment (`REST_MS`) before the next line, cut or question; a question is asked again only after `NUDGE_MS` and then less and less often; the sound of a tapped beat comes a little after the tap's click (`TAP_GAP_MS`), and taps on nothing play a note at most every `FREE_NOTE_MS`. In a film's data: few sounds per beat (one ring or siren where three were, at most three, 0.9 s apart as played), enough time for every line (`films.test.ts`), slow walks and drives, loops that pulse gently (a blue light or a ringing phone sways, never flickers), and choice cards that come out one by one, the next only once the line of the last one has been said. When something feels busy, take a sound or a move out rather than make it quicker.
- **Every tap spot and option lies wholly inside the scene's `focus`**, and nothing that matters lies under the strip: on a wide phone only the focus is sure to be seen (`films.test.ts`). Grow the focus rather than move a thing to the edge.
- **`scenes(picks)` keeps its prefix**: a pick only adds scenes after the ones played (the test checks it). A scene's `Draw` is a component at module level – never made inside a function (Biome's `noComponentHookFactories`, and it would remount with every render); a scene that depends on a pick reads it from `picks`.
- **Show the thing before the tap that uses it.** The warning triangle is carried and seen before the child taps where it goes; a tap must never make something appear from nowhere.
- **People face the way they walk** (`face` -1 / 1 on `Grownup`): the crew walking left to the pole was once drawn looking right.
- **The child stays seen while things happen round it** (found in „Beim Bäcker“): nothing that moves – a tipping sack,
  an opening door, a carried bag – passes over its face, so leave it room beside what it works on. Behind a counter or
  table it stands so that its chest shows above the top (its feet hidden below it), never with its feet on the top, or it
  seems to stand on the table. What waits to be tapped (a spade, a seed packet) lies beside the child, never in front
  of its legs; where a plant grows tall or wide, the child stands clear of it (the garden's harvest moves it to the
  bed's corner, `HARVEST_KID_X`: the carrot tops hid its face). Something the child eats or drinks lies in front of its
  face with its tip at the mouth (`../kid.tsx` draws it after the head): held in the hand at the mouth it was behind the
  head and could not be seen. A window in a door, an oven or a box is a hole in that thing's drawing (`fillRule="evenodd"`)
  with see-through glass over it, so what is inside (the browning bread, the glow) shows through.
- **Places look finished, like a picture book, never like boxes** (feedback on „Die Feuerwehr“): a house has a door
  with a step, framed windows with sills and a chimney; a tree a crown of several leaf balls with light on it; a
  vehicle wheel arches, lights, a shadow; a room is furnished (the station: curtains, a picture, a plant, lamps, tiles,
  boots under the jackets, warning stripes at the gate); the sky has clouds and the sun its rays; where a road ends,
  the meadow goes on after it. All of it still – nothing moves that the story doesn't move. When improving a drawing,
  keep where things stand (tap spots, paths and `focus` refer to them) and add the detail around them.
- **A choice first shows what each way is, then asks** (user feedback, November 2026: a yellow ring round each thing in
  the town was dull and didn't say that these are three stories to pick from). Each option comes out as its own
  card, a frame of film with a close-up of the trouble as the child just saw it, one after the other with its sound and
  line, and only then the question – never only a ring or glow round things in the picture. A new option gets its
  `card` (a close-up, not the strip's small icon) and its `sounds`; both choices of a film use the same close-ups, so
  the cat on the radio's card is the cat from the town.
- **Things are handled as they really are, and a film of something being made shows the hands doing each step**
  (user feedback on „Ein Brief für Oma“ and „Beim Bäcker“, November 2026): a stamp goes on the envelope's front with
  the address, never on the back with its flap – so the envelope turns over first; the dough is kneaded by the child's
  hands pressing and folding it, never a ball that only wobbles by itself. Before drawing a step, think how it is
  really done and which side or part of the thing it is done to; where the thing is bigger than the child's reach,
  make it smaller there (the fresh dough is, and grows under the cloth) rather than stretch the child's arms.
- **Every choice is right, and each option is what it shows**: picking is never a test. At most a choice at the start and one in the middle, so a film stays a few minutes.
- **A scene's `focus` is as low as its story allows**: on a phone on its side the room is wide and low, so a tall focus
  makes everyone tiny. A scene with the sky in it (the garden's rain and sun, the sun fixed in `Outdoors` at 1420, 110)
  takes the sky only as far as its story needs.
- „The ambulance“ (G-19, `krankenwagen.tsx`, English first, `Ambulance` drawing exported): at the park the child picks how the little bear fell – off its bike (sore knee) or off its scooter (sore elbow) → at
  home the fox parent rings the ambulance (`call-<fall>`) → at the station the paramedic (a fox, `Grownup wear="rescue"`) and the driver (a bear) board it → the drive with siren and blue lights → at the park the bag lies on the ground
  beside the paramedic, the child taps it open, then the plaster goes on the sore spot, then a high five and everyone cheers → back home. Calm and harmless; no phrasebook entries yet, so every language hears English.
- „From grain to bread“ (G-20, `korn.tsx`, English first, classes `fm-kn-`): on the farm the fox farmer and the child
  sow the field with the tractor and its seed drill (seeds fall into the furrows as it passes) → the child fetches the
  rain cloud, green shoots come up, it pushes the cloud from the sun and the grain grows green, then golden → the
  combine harvester cuts the grain stretch by stretch (stubble stays) and pours it from its pipe into the trailer, and

  miller (a she-bear) goes in and comes out with a sack in a puff of flour, and it goes on the trailer → at the bakery

- „We build a house“ (G-36, `haus.tsx`, English first, classes `fm-hs-`): on the building site the fox digs the hole
  with the digger, the bear's mixer pours the slab, and the child picks the house's colour (red, yellow, blue: the
  bricks of every later scene) → the builder (a she-bear) lays three rows of bricks, one tap on her pallet each →
  the vixen's tower crane lifts the roof, swings it over the house and lowers it, a tap puts on the tiles → the van
  backs up, the child opens it, carries the teddy in and switches on the light → a party in front of the new house with
  everyone who built it: the cake is cut and the child rings the new doorbell.
- „The rubbish truck“ (G-27, `muell.tsx`, English first, classes `fm-mu-`): the child picks which bin goes out today
  (grey with rubbish bags, blue with paper, yellow with plastic) → it pushes the bin to the street with its grown-up,
  and the rubbish truck backs up slowly with one short beep → the truck's arm lifts the bin and tips it over the
  hopper, what lay in it falls in, the arm sets it down empty, a tap on the green button lets the press squeeze it with
  a soft whirr, and the truck (a bear driving) drives on → at the recycling yard the child takes the glass, the paper
  and the plastic out of a crate, each into its own container. No horn, no siren.
- A figure that walks in a scene is mounted a step before it walks: `Go` only places a thing on its first path, so a
  figure that first appears with its walking path stands at the end at once (the family of „We build a house“ never
  walked to the door). Mount it with its start, then change the path; a thing that is only seen while it moves (the
  things flying into the yard's containers in „The rubbish truck“) keeps its `Go` from the start with nothing in it.
- To play a film through in the preview, send keys instead of clicking: a digit picks that card (only once the cards
  are out – sent earlier, nothing happens) and any other key taps the spot shown; a click on the picture while the
  cards come out picks the next card in turn, not the one under it.
- **New films and their lines come in English first** (`Film.first: "en"`, since „The ambulance“, 16 November 2026; the older German-first films stay German): the title goes through `titleIn(title, language, "en")`, the voice is English until the title is translated (`voice` in `FilmPlay.tsx`), and a line without a translation is said in English (`spokenIn(text, language, first)`, every `first` parameter defaults to `"de"`). Their lines are never recorded or translated on your own (German too is a translation made on request); `recordedLines.ts` records an English-first line in other languages only via `translationOf(line, language, "en")`, never as German, and the management list shows them under `ENGLISH_FILMS`. „Die Feuerwehr“, „Ein Brief für Oma“ and „Beim Bäcker“ are translated into every app language; `../phrases.test.ts` checks a film is translated whole or not at all.
