# Spaß im Aufzug

Route `/aufzug/` · idea N-37, in „Lebendige Stories“.

Picture stories of about two minutes in a house in cross-section with four floors and a lift. A thought bubble above the child shows what it needs next (the same drawing as the thing in the room), and the child rides the lift to that floor – big buttons 1–4 right of the house, a tap on the room, or the number keys and arrows. A wrong floor: the child looks out, shakes its head, a light runs along the outline of the bubble and the right room starts to glow; after a while without a tap the same happens again. In the room the first thing happens by itself; then the big action button left of the house lights up whenever the child can do something (a tap on it, anywhere in the picture, or any key). Three stories, picked on the start screen and again on picture cards at the end (`content.ts`): „Pfannkuchen“ (hungry: chef's hat, eggs from the fridge in the cellar, batter and pancakes in the kitchen, fetch the little brother, his juice, eat together on the roof terrace), „Gute Nacht“ (bath, pyjama, warm milk, brushing teeth, teddy, bed – the house goes dark) and „Pfützen“ (rain: boots, raincoat, umbrella, rain hat, paper boat, jump in the garden puddles until the rainbow comes). The children with their poses and clothes and the thought bubble are in `kid.tsx`, the things in `things.tsx`, the ten rooms in `rooms.tsx`, house, lift, floor buttons and the action button in `house.tsx`, sizes in `layout.ts`; the story runs as a script of waits in `ElevatorPlay.tsx`. All drawn in SVG, no emojis, nothing to read.

## Rules for every story (from feedback; follow them in new stories too)

- **The first thing in a room happens by itself.** Getting out of the lift, the child does the first move on its own (puts the hat on, opens the fridge, sits down) – the ride was the child's tap. A step whose whole point is one move needs no tap at all.
- **At most two taps in a row**, and several taps only where the child can count what each one does: two eggs, two boots, one tap per pancake. Between runs something happens by itself (`auto` task, a `wait`, or a walk to a new `spot`). `content.test.ts` checks this.
- **One action button, always in the same place** (left of the house, big and bright, glowing while it waits). Nothing is framed or pointed at in the picture.
- **Only wish for what can be seen.** The thing in the bubble stands visibly in its room – nothing in a cupboard, nothing to find in a second place, no trip to fetch something forgotten.
- **Things stand out from their room.** A thing to use has a colour (and a dark outline) that differs from the wall, worktop or furniture behind it – no blue bowl in front of blue cupboards, no white bag on white tiles.
- **Show what happens.** What goes into a bowl, a pot or a puddle stays visible (glass bowl, layers, the mixer in the batter), not hidden behind the thing.
- **The thought bubble pops up once per wish.** Reminders don't pop it again; a light runs along its outline instead.
