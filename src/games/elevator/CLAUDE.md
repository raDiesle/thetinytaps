# Spaß im Aufzug

Route `/aufzug/` · idea N-37, in „Lebendige Stories“.

Picture stories of about two minutes in a house in cross-section with four floors and a lift. A thought bubble above the child shows what it needs next (the same drawing as the thing in the room), and the child rides the lift to that floor – big buttons 1–4 beside the house, a tap on the room, or the number keys and arrows. A wrong floor: the child looks out, shakes its head and the right room starts to glow; after a while without a tap the bubble pops up again and the room glows too. Three stories, picked on the start screen and again on picture cards at the end (`content.ts`): „Pfannkuchen“ (hungry: fridge in the cellar, pancakes in the kitchen, fetch the little brother, his juice, eat together on the roof terrace), „Gute Nacht“ (bath, warm milk, brushing teeth, teddy, bed – the house goes dark) and „Pfützen“ (rain: boots, raincoat, umbrella, paper boat, jump in the garden puddles until the rainbow comes). The children with their poses and clothes and the thought bubble are in `kid.tsx`, the things in `things.tsx`, the ten rooms in `rooms.tsx`, house, lift and buttons in `house.tsx`, sizes in `layout.ts`; the story runs as a script of waits in `ElevatorPlay.tsx`. All drawn in SVG, no emojis, nothing to read.
