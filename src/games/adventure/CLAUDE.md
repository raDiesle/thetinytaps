# Bewegungsabenteuer

Route `/abenteuer/`.

A 3D movement story (three.js, loaded only on this page). The fox Tapsi shows each movement, the child does it along in front of the screen, and the world answers on its own time – there is no camera and no text, Tapsi says everything. The movement library (`library/`: categories, 105 movements, worlds, scenarios, sequences, quality notes in `library/README.md`) is kept apart from the 3D story (`stories/treasureIsland/`, „Die Schatzinsel“, about 6 minutes: `build()` and the moving in `index.ts`, the steps and their timing in `script.ts`, the props in `scenery.ts`, put together in `world.ts`, positions along the path in `layout.ts`): it opens with a red dotted trail drawing the way to the chest like on a treasure map, and at every station Tapsi first shows the movement alone, then the child joins in. Tapsi always faces the island and the camera looks from behind, so the child sees what Tapsi sees and left is left; where arms in front of the body would be hidden (rope, key) it moves round to the side and up; it films the stone jumps from the side like a jump-and-run, and goes centered and low behind Tapsi when things roll at it. After the trail the camera swings round in front of Tapsi, so the child sees its face first, then goes behind. Tapsi starts standing on Ole: it waves to Kiki, casts a fishing rod over the right shoulder (the paws go where the rod is, beside the head), then sits down and later stands up again as steps of their own, so the child does it too; it paddles sitting. Every movement does something to a thing in front of Tapsi: fish leaping over the float, waving to Kiki, the paddle, scorpions, a star box over every jump to bump with the head (Ole walks fast enough that each jump has its own), the log, two hanging drums whose every beat scares a bird off the path, the crank, coconuts the monkeys throw down (passing right where Tapsi stood: a big step aside, then from the left under the lifted left leg, then through the straddled legs), the riddle door (twice round), cobwebs, the rope (pulled in hand over hand: the raft moves only while a paw pulls, `pulledIn` in `motion.ts`, and the rope runs from the paws past the body's side onto the ground), the key in the lock, and at the end the crabs carry the open chest up the beach, Tapsi hops down off Ole, reaches deep into it and throws the coins up high with both arms (a rain of gold instead of a dance; `CARRY`, `tossAt` in `script.ts`). Every step's clip is a whole number of goes and each go has its target (`CYCLES` in `script.ts`, checked by `treasureIsland.test.ts`), so nothing is left over at the end of a movement. Paws hold what they turn (`guide.hold` for paddle, rod, crank bar, key ring), and Ole walks from the cobwebs through the gorge into the bay – no cut, no flash. The fox is a posable rig (`three/guide.ts`) with one motion clip per movement (`three/clips/`: one file per body part, which decides what glows; building blocks and timings in `motion.ts`); `three/player.ts` plays a story on a clock (arrow keys skip, Space pauses), `three/stage.ts` has renderer, sky, light and water. The fox has a thin ink outline, and only the body part a movement is about (e.g. the arms at the crank) gets a wide glowing one while it moves (`three/contour.ts`, inverted hulls). A dark child figure with a glowing rim like in "Just Dance" (`three/silhouette.ts`) shows every movement too, seen from the same side as the camera sees the fox. In development `__adventure.seek(seconds)` in the console jumps to a moment.

The game has no settings of its own: read aloud, sounds and its own music follow the shared options on the home page (`useCommonSettings`), the glow and the child figure are always on. The start screen only has „Über das Spiel“.

## Working on levels

- Read `LEVEL-REGELN.md` in this folder first and check the work against it. Every piece of feedback
  on a level goes into it as a general rule (German), in the same commit as the fix.
- The movement library has its own notes in `library/README.md`; `library/library.test.ts` checks that
  scenarios and sequences only use movements that exist.
- `__adventure.seek(seconds)` in the console jumps to a moment; arrow keys skip, Space pauses.
- three.js is only imported below this folder, so the rest of the app stays small.
