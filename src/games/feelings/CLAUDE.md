# Gefühle

Route `/gefuehle/`.

Comfort the bear (`bear.ts`, `BearPlay.tsx`) and kindergarten stories – pushed, kicked, toy taken, slide full, wanting a toy, angry yourself (`kita.ts`, `KitaPlay.tsx`); the animals in `Critter.tsx`, backgrounds in `places.tsx`; every choice is a picture card (`picture` in the content, drawn by `ChoiceCard` in `shared.tsx`) with a green tick or red cross, so children can choose without reading; illustrations dropped into `bilder/` replace the drawn pictures (file names and image prompts in `bilder/README.md`)

Illustrations are made with `/bild-erzeugen` (`yarn image`); the list of pictures and their prompts is in `bilder/README.md`.
