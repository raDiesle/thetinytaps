# Kettenreaktion

Route `/kettenreaktion/` (the old address `/kugelbahn/` redirects).

Eight levels in turn (or only the machines, or one of them); after each one „Nochmal“ or „Weiter“ (any key goes on). The marble run (`TrackLevel.tsx`, route and layout in `track.ts`): one tap or key opens the hopper, and the marbles come out one after the other; each rolls downhill over a xylophone and through three spots (bell, funnel, paddle wheel), rides the seesaw, goes through the tunnel into the basket – a full basket ends the level. Tapping a spot swaps its part (bell, trampoline, funnel, paddle wheel; the route comes in sections, one per part and spot), and the next marble takes the new way; a setting turns this off. „Was rollt denn da?“ (K-07, a setting, on by default): instead of six marbles in a row, a shelf over the first row holds three of five things (marble, tennis ball, iron ball, apple, soap bubble; `ROLLER` in `track.ts`); tapping one flies it into the hopper and something else pops up in its place, a key or a tap on the wall takes them in turn. Each rolls its own way on the same route: the tennis ball hops along the rows, the iron ball is slow with deep notes and thumps, the apple bumps, the soap bubble floats at an even speed and bursts above the basket instead of landing (so it doesn't fill it). "Alles fällt um" (`ToppleLevel.tsx`): everyday things (`things.tsx`) stand small to tall and topple onto each other like dominoes until the drum; each round picks other things. "Große Maschine" (`MachineLevel.tsx`, layout and timing in `machine.ts`): a Rube Goldberg machine through a 6900 px long playroom – one tap, and for about 30 seconds one thing sets off the next (marble, xylophone, bell, funnel, ten dominoes, a toy car through a loop onto a seesaw catapult, a beach ball down block stairs into a bucket, which pulls up a gate for the train, which presses the button for the jack-in-the-box); the camera follows whatever moves. Everything is a function of the seconds since the tap; in development `__machine.seek(seconds)` in the console jumps to a moment. Five more Rube Goldberg machines run the same way, each with its own world and no element repeated from another: „Verrückte Küche" (`kitchen.ts`, `KitchenLevel.tsx`), „Im Garten" (`garden.ts`), „Baustelle" (`building.ts`), „Zirkus" (`circus.ts`), „Raketenstart" (`space.ts`). They share the helpers in `machineKit.ts` (paths, rolling, hops, timeline, camera), the stage in `MachineStage.tsx` (clock, sounds, camera, tap) and wall pictures in `scenery.tsx`; in development `?level=garden` in the address opens one level directly.

## Working on machines

- Everything in a machine level is a pure function of the seconds since the tap, so any moment can be
  looked at with `__machine.seek(seconds)` in the console; `?level=<id>` opens one level directly.
- Each machine has its own world: never reuse an element of another machine.
