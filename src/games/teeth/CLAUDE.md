# Zähne putzen

Route `/zaehne/`.

Brushing timer in seven steps, as long as its song (no choice of minutes: the sung song's own length, about two minutes), in KAI order (chewing surfaces, outside, inside). It starts at the sink (`prep.tsx`, can be turned off): a hand takes the brush out of its cup, the tube puts a blob of toothpaste on it. A small 3D mouth model (`jaw.ts`) shows each step from the right angle – mouth open or teeth together, head turned to the side, the jaw being brushed tipped to show its top or tongue side. Arrows on the lips show when to open or close the mouth. The brush is a small 3D model too (`brush.tsx`), held as dentists teach it for each side (flat on the chewing surfaces, in the cheek pocket with the handle out of the corner of the mouth, tipped towards the gum inside) and glides along the row scrubbing, circling or wiping from gum to tooth. Sugar monsters sit on every tooth not brushed yet; the ones to brush now glow, and each vanishes as the brush reaches it. The mirror fills the whole screen: `useFocusBox` (`mouth.tsx`) zooms so all teeth with their monsters just fit and shows more of the face in the leftover room, so on a phone the mouth is as wide as the screen. The sung song always plays, no tapping needed, keeps the screen on; arrow keys skip steps, Space or the ⏸️ button next to the progress bar pauses, and tapping or dragging on the progress bar jumps to that point (also while paused; the bar sits above the pause screen). Settings (`tinytaps.teeth.v3`) are only the prelude and the week chart; speech and sounds come from the home page.

Song (`music.ts`): `musik/musik-zaehneputzen-lied-<lang>.mp3`, one verse per step singing what the picture shows („Mund weit auf … hin und her“), the eighth part „Fertig! Blitzeblank“ on the end screen. It plays at the brushing time (from its first verse), so it follows pause and jumps. The voice says nothing during the steps and at the end, the song is the hint; with „Vorlesen“ on the home page only the prelude is spoken, and the song gets quiet while the voice speaks (it watches `speechSynthesis.speaking`). Each step lasts as long as its verse (`sungSteps`, from the .json next to the song: where each verse begins), so the brushing lasts as long as the song (German 1:53, English 2:11); everything goes by `stepStarts`. Until the file has loaded (or when it can't be), the synthesized `playTuneNote` tune plays and, with „Vorlesen“ on, the voice says the steps. Nothing plays during the prelude. The shared background song stays off here.

The song is sung in the Spielsprache of the home page (`useLanguages`, the language of the app's texts), German when there is no song in it (`sungLanguage`). A language is just its two files: every `musik/musik-zaehneputzen-lied-<lang>.mp3` with its `.json` is found by its name (`sung.ts`); the lyrics for Suno are in `musik/suno-lied.md`. A new language: `/zahnputzlied`.

Morning or evening (`daytime.ts`, by the clock: until 14:00 is the morning, no choice): in the morning the sun rises in the window at the sink and in the corner, and breakfast crumbs stick to the chin until all is clean; in the evening the moon is out, the sleeve is a pyjama, the sky turns dusk and the end says „Gute Nacht“. Every finished brushing puts a star on a week chart (`week.tsx`, Monday to Sunday, a sun and a moon slot per day), saved under `tinytaps.teeth.week.v1` for two weeks and shown before brushing and at the end next to „Nochmal“, never while brushing (can be turned off).

## Rules

- The song is the brushing, not background music: it sings the steps and sets how long they last, so it always plays, also when "🎵 Musik" is off on the home page, and there is no "plain music" or "no song" choice.
- What the app already decides is never a setting here: the song's language is the Spielsprache of the home page (the language of the texts), speech is „Vorlesen“ of the home page, the daytime is the clock. Never add a second choice for the same thing.
- Setting labels are short and start with an emoji („🪥 Vorher: Bürste holen, Zahnpasta drauf“); explanations go into one `setting-note` below.
