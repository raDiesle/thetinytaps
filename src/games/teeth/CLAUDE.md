# Zähne putzen

Route `/zaehne/`.

Brushing timer in seven steps, as long as its song (no choice of minutes: two minutes with the plain song, the sung song's own length with it), in KAI order (chewing surfaces, outside, inside). It starts with a short story of where the sugar monsters come from (`origin.tsx`, about 10 s, can be turned off): sweets fly into a big grin and leave sugar crumbs on the teeth, then the sun crosses the sky (evening: the day goes by) or the moon does and the sun comes up (morning: the night goes by) while each crumb grows into a monster. Then the sink (`prep.tsx`, can be turned off): a hand takes the brush out of its cup, the tube puts a blob of toothpaste on it. A small 3D mouth model (`jaw.ts`) shows each step from the right angle – mouth open or teeth together, head turned to the side, the jaw being brushed tipped to show its top or tongue side. Arrows on the lips show when to open or close the mouth. The brush is a small 3D model too (`brush.tsx`), held as dentists teach it for each side (flat on the chewing surfaces, in the cheek pocket with the handle out of the corner of the mouth, tipped towards the gum inside) and glides along the row scrubbing, circling or wiping from gum to tooth. Sugar monsters sit on every tooth not brushed yet, from the first look in the mirror; the ones to brush now glow, and each vanishes as the brush reaches it. The mirror (and the story and the sink before it) fills the whole screen behind the heading and the controls: `useRoom` measures `.teeth-room`, the free part between them, and `fitBox` (`mouth.tsx`) zooms so what matters just fits there and the rest of the screen shows more face or wall around it. For the mouth that is the lips with the arrows on them (`mouthFocus`), taken from the view of the moment, so a closed mouth or one seen from the side zooms in as far as an open one; the frame keeps its thickness in px. On a phone on its side the week chart waits for the end, so the sink keeps the room. Music (on by default) and spoken hints (off by default, own switch independent of the home page), no tapping needed, keeps the screen on; arrow keys skip steps, Space or the ⏸️ button next to the progress bar pauses, and tapping or dragging on the progress bar jumps to that point (also while paused; the bar sits above the pause screen). Settings are `tinytaps.teeth.v3` since music became on and speech off by default.

Music (`music.ts`): `musik/musik-zaehneputzen.mp3`, a Suno instrumental at 112 BPM with a new part every 8 bars = 17.14 s, exactly one step of the two-minute brushing. It plays at the brushing time (from its first beat, `PLAIN.start`), so it follows pause and jumps, and each step begins with a new part. When the brushing is done the eighth part plays on once to the final chord (`PLAIN.finale`), on the end screen. It gets quiet while the voice speaks (it watches `speechSynthesis.speaking`). Without the file, or until it has loaded, the synthesized `playTuneNote` tune plays. It begins with the first step. Before that, while the brush is fetched, the shared background song (`musik-hintergrund.mp3`) runs on from the menu; from the first step it is hushed (`useNoBackgroundMusic`), and back in the menu it goes on where it stopped. The last three seconds before the brushing are counted down big (3, 2, 1, under a drum roll that gets louder each second, `playDrumRoll`), and the brushing begins with „Los!“ and a „Tusch“ (`playTusch`) instead of the chime; during the whole prelude the clock in the corner shows the time until the start (🪥 0:19) instead of the brushing time. It plays only while "🎵 Musik" is on for all games on the home page too, and the shared background song stays off while it plays.

Sung song (setting „Gesungen“, German by default): `musik/musik-zaehneputzen-lied-de.mp3` plays instead, one verse per step singing what the picture shows („Mund weit auf … hin und her“), the eighth part „Fertig! Blitzeblank“ on the end screen. While it plays the voice says nothing during the steps and at the end, the song is the hint (the prelude is still spoken). With the sung song each step lasts as long as its verse (`sungSteps` in `music.ts`, from the .json next to the song: where each verse begins), so the brushing lasts as long as the song in that language (German 1:53, English 2:11); everything that went by `stepMs` goes by `stepStarts` instead. A language is just its two files: every `musik/musik-zaehneputzen-lied-<lang>.mp3` with its `.json` appears in the choice by its name (`sung.ts`); the lyrics for Suno are in `musik/suno-lied.md`. A new language: `/zahnputzlied`.

Morning or evening (`daytime.ts`, by the clock until 14:00 unless the parent picks one): in the morning the sun rises in the window at the sink and in the corner, and breakfast crumbs stick to the chin until all is clean; in the evening the moon is out, the sleeve is a pyjama, the sky turns dusk and the end says „Gute Nacht“. Every finished brushing puts a star on a week chart (`week.tsx`, Monday to Sunday, a sun and a moon slot per day), saved under `tinytaps.teeth.week.v1` for two weeks and shown before brushing and at the end next to „Menü“ and „Nochmal“ (there is no next level, so no „Weiter“), never while brushing (can be turned off).

## Rules

- The monsters grow only in the story at the start. In the mirror they are already on the teeth: never let them pop up or arrive there (they only fade in softly as a side of the teeth turns into view), that would be a second, illogical origin.
- A monster sits on its tooth, not over its neighbours: keep `MONSTER_SIZE` (`mouth.tsx`) small, and the story's monsters about a tooth wide.
- The story is short (two parts of about 5 s each): it explains, the brushing is the game.
