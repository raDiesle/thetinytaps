# Zähne putzen

Route `/zaehne/`.

Brushing timer in seven steps, as long as its song (no choice of minutes: two minutes with the plain song, the sung song's own length with it), in KAI order (chewing surfaces, outside, inside). It starts with sweets and then the sink (can be turned off together). `treat.tsx`: the child sees a lollipop, a candy and a cookie on a plate and beams (hearts, „Yay!“), its hand brings each to the mouth, it bites (crumbs fly, a bite is missing), chews with full cheeks and closed eyes, hums „Mmmh“ and grins – with two sugar monsters on its teeth, which leads to the brushing. The scene is drawn from the brushing clock (`at`), so it follows pause and jumps. `prep.tsx`: a hand takes the brush out of its cup (it clinks), the tube squeezes a striped stripe of toothpaste along the bristles. Their sounds are `Cue`s (`TREAT_CUES`, `PREP_CUES`, ms from the start of the part) that `TeethPlay` fires when the clock passes them, skipped on a jump; most are CC0 recordings (yay, wrapper, lolly, munch, chew, yum, clink) with a synth fallback, preloaded when the prelude is on. `prelude.test.ts` checks the cues lie inside their part and the files exist. A small 3D mouth model (`jaw.ts`) shows each step from the right angle – mouth open or teeth together, head turned to the side, the jaw being brushed tipped to show its top or tongue side. Arrows on the lips show when to open or close the mouth. The teeth grow out of the gum: the gum line (`GUM_LINE` in `jaw.ts`) rises into a papilla between neighbouring teeth and arches down over each crown, the faces of each tooth end there and the gum's rim follows it. The brush is a small 3D model too (`brush.tsx`: white and light-blue bristle tufts with a longer tip row, a rounded head, thin neck, green rubber grip; it rocks a little on its handle while scrubbing or circling, and the foam grows over the first half minute), held as dentists teach it for each side (flat on the chewing surfaces, in the cheek pocket with the handle out of the corner of the mouth, tipped towards the gum inside) and glides along the row scrubbing, circling or wiping from gum to tooth. Sugar monsters sit on every tooth not brushed yet; the ones to brush now glow, and each vanishes as the brush reaches it. The mirror fills the whole screen: `useFocusBox` (`mouth.tsx`) zooms so all teeth with their monsters just fit and shows more of the face in the leftover room, so on a phone the mouth is as wide as the screen. Music (on by default) and spoken hints (off by default, own switch independent of the home page), no tapping needed, keeps the screen on; arrow keys skip steps, Space or the ⏸️ button next to the progress bar pauses, and tapping or dragging on the progress bar jumps to that point (also while paused; the bar sits above the pause screen). Settings are `tinytaps.teeth.v3` since music became on and speech off by default.

Music (`music.ts`): `musik/musik-zaehneputzen.mp3`, a Suno instrumental at 112 BPM with a new part every 8 bars = 17.14 s, exactly one step of the two-minute brushing. It plays at the brushing time (from its first beat, `PLAIN.start`), so it follows pause and jumps, and each step begins with a new part. When the brushing is done the eighth part plays on once to the final chord (`PLAIN.finale`), on the end screen. It gets quiet while the voice speaks (it watches `speechSynthesis.speaking`). Without the file, or until it has loaded, the synthesized `playTuneNote` tune plays. Nothing plays during the prelude. It plays only while "🎵 Musik" is on for all games on the home page too, and the shared background song stays off here.

Sung song (setting „Gesungen“, German by default): `musik/musik-zaehneputzen-lied-de.mp3` plays instead, one verse per step singing what the picture shows („Mund weit auf … hin und her“), the eighth part „Fertig! Blitzeblank“ on the end screen. While it plays the voice says nothing during the steps and at the end, the song is the hint (the prelude is still spoken). With the sung song each step lasts as long as its verse (`sungSteps` in `music.ts`, from the .json next to the song: where each verse begins), so the brushing lasts as long as the song in that language (German 1:53, English 2:11); everything that went by `stepMs` goes by `stepStarts` instead. A language is just its two files: every `musik/musik-zaehneputzen-lied-<lang>.mp3` with its `.json` appears in the choice by its name (`sung.ts`); the lyrics for Suno are in `musik/suno-lied.md`. A new language: `/zahnputzlied`.

Morning or evening (`daytime.ts`, by the clock until 14:00 unless the parent picks one): in the morning the sun rises in the window at the sink and in the corner, and breakfast crumbs stick to the chin until all is clean; in the evening the moon is out, the sleeve is a pyjama, the sky turns dusk and the end says „Gute Nacht“. Every finished brushing puts a star on a week chart (`week.tsx`, Monday to Sunday, a sun and a moon slot per day), saved under `tinytaps.teeth.week.v1` for two weeks and shown before brushing and at the end next to „Nochmal“, never while brushing (can be turned off).
