# Zähne putzen

Route `/zaehne/`.

Brushing timer (1–3 minutes) in seven steps in KAI order (chewing surfaces, outside, inside). It starts at the sink (`prep.tsx`, can be turned off): a hand takes the brush out of its cup, the tube puts a blob of toothpaste on it. A small 3D mouth model (`jaw.ts`) shows each step from the right angle – mouth open or teeth together, head turned to the side, the jaw being brushed tipped to show its top or tongue side. Arrows on the lips show when to open or close the mouth. The brush is a small 3D model too (`brush.tsx`), held as dentists teach it for each side (flat on the chewing surfaces, in the cheek pocket with the handle out of the corner of the mouth, tipped towards the gum inside) and glides along the row scrubbing, circling or wiping from gum to tooth. Sugar monsters sit on every tooth not brushed yet; the ones to brush now glow, and each vanishes as the brush reaches it. The mirror fills the whole screen: `useFocusBox` (`mouth.tsx`) zooms so all teeth with their monsters just fit and shows more of the face in the leftover room, so on a phone the mouth is as wide as the screen. Music (on by default) and spoken hints (off by default, own switch independent of the home page), no tapping needed, keeps the screen on; arrow keys skip steps, Space or the ⏸️ button next to the progress bar pauses, and tapping or dragging on the progress bar jumps to that point (also while paused; the bar sits above the pause screen). Settings are `tinytaps.teeth.v3` since music became on and speech off by default.

Music (`music.ts`): `musik/musik-zaehneputzen.mp3`, a Suno instrumental at 112 BPM with a new part every 8 bars = 17.14 s, exactly one step of the two-minute brushing. It plays at the brushing time (from its first beat, `SONG.start`), so it follows pause and jumps, and in two-minute mode each step begins with a new part; other lengths go round the first eight parts. When the brushing is done the eighth part plays on once to the final chord (`SONG.finale`), on the end screen. It gets quiet while the voice speaks (it watches `speechSynthesis.speaking`). Without the file, or until it has loaded, the synthesized `playTuneNote` tune plays. Nothing plays during the prelude. It plays only while "🎵 Musik" is on for all games on the home page too, and the shared background song stays off here.

Sung song (setting „Gesungen“, German by default): in two-minute brushing `musik/musik-zaehneputzen-lied-de.mp3` plays instead, one verse per step singing what the picture shows („Mund weit auf … hin und her“), the eighth part „Fertig! Blitzeblank“ on the end screen. While it plays the voice says nothing during the steps and at the end, the song is the hint (the prelude is still spoken). 1 and 3 minutes keep the plain song, the verses wouldn't fit. A language appears in the choice once its file is in `musik/` and in `SUNG` (`music.ts`); the lyrics for Suno are in `musik/suno-lied.md`.

Morning or evening (`daytime.ts`, by the clock until 14:00 unless the parent picks one): in the morning the sun rises in the window at the sink and in the corner, and breakfast crumbs stick to the chin until all is clean; in the evening the moon is out, the sleeve is a pyjama, the sky turns dusk and the end says „Gute Nacht“. Every finished brushing puts a star on a week chart (`week.tsx`, Monday to Sunday, a sun and a moon slot per day), saved under `tinytaps.teeth.week.v1` for two weeks and shown before brushing and at the end next to „Nochmal“, never while brushing (can be turned off).
