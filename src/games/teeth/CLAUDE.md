# Zähne putzen

Route `/zaehne/`.

Brushing timer (1–3 minutes) in seven steps in KAI order (chewing surfaces, outside, inside). It starts at the sink (`prep.tsx`, can be turned off): a hand takes the brush out of its cup, the tube puts a blob of toothpaste on it. A small 3D mouth model (`jaw.ts`) shows each step from the right angle – mouth open or teeth together, head turned to the side, the jaw being brushed tipped to show its top or tongue side. Arrows on the lips show when to open or close the mouth. The brush is a small 3D model too (`brush.tsx`), held as dentists teach it for each side (flat on the chewing surfaces, in the cheek pocket with the handle out of the corner of the mouth, tipped towards the gum inside) and glides along the row scrubbing, circling or wiping from gum to tooth. Sugar monsters sit on every tooth not brushed yet; the ones to brush now glow, and each vanishes as the brush reaches it. Music (on by default) and spoken hints, no tapping needed, keeps the screen on; arrow keys skip steps, Space or the ⏸️ button next to the progress bar pauses, and tapping or dragging on the progress bar jumps to that point (also while paused; the bar sits above the pause screen). Settings are `tinytaps.teeth.v2` since music became on by default.

Morning or evening (`daytime.ts`, by the clock until 14:00 unless the parent picks one): in the morning the sun rises in the window at the sink and in the corner, and breakfast crumbs stick to the chin until all is clean; in the evening the moon is out, the sleeve is a pyjama, the sky turns dusk and the end says „Gute Nacht“. Every finished brushing puts a star on a week chart (`week.tsx`, Monday to Sunday, a sun and a moon slot per day), saved under `tinytaps.teeth.week.v1` for two weeks and shown at the end next to „Nochmal“ (can be turned off).
